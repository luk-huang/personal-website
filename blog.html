Is Anybody Doing Async RL Correctly?

May 2026

Large-scale post-training systems are moving to async RL because it can deliver 2-3x faster training, but stale trajectories turn the update into a fragile off-policy estimator. This post explains why masking and clipping only partially help, and why variance control matters at high policy lag.
